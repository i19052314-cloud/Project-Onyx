# Onyx Postal Map — credits

The postal map tiles and radar files in the Onyx Postal Map packs (Legacy and Enhanced) are the postal map by
JnKTechstuff and Jeff Favignano, released by OCRP as "OCRP Postal and Minimap (Server Side)" for others to use
(cfx.re FiveM Releases, 2020-01-27): https://forum.cfx.re/t/ocrp-postal-and-minimap-server-side/992775
Source: https://github.com/ocrp/postal_map

The Legacy pack carries those files as released. The Enhanced pack carries the same images converted to the
Enhanced texture format. The minimap zoom levels Onyx sets while the postal map is on follow that release's
client script. The release states no licence; credit is given here to its authors and to OCRP.
