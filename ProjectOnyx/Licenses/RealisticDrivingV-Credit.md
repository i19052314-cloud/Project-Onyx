# Realistic Vehicles — credits

Project Onyx's "Realistic Vehicles" setting (F10, World) is a C++ rewrite of the FiveM "FiveM Realistic Vehicles" resource collection (RealisticDrivingV, RealisticTraffic, RealisticAirControl, RealisticBrakeLights) on the Script Hook V SDK. No Lua code is used.

## Realistic Driving V — handling data

`Data/RealisticHandling.xml` is the vehicle handling data of **Realistic Driving V** by **Killatomate** (https://www.gta5-mods.com/vehicles/realistic-driving-v), as shipped in the FiveM collection. All credit for the handling research and values goes to Killatomate.

## FiveM Realistic Vehicles

`Data/RealisticAiHandling.xml` (AI driving curves) and the traffic density, brake-light and mid-air control rules come from the FiveM Realistic Vehicles collection, which credits Realistic Driving V for its handling.
