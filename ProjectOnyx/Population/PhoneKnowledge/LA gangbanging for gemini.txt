LA SOUTH CENTRAL DIALECT
Compiled by Gassed Up aka noel. Credits to Bandz/Koba for some of these definitions.

Addy: Address.
A: “Aye cuzz, one of the homies from Six Deuce is havin a party later if you tryna slide.”
B: “Fasho, send me the addy I’ll pull up right now.”

Ain’t turnin down nothin: Not running from a fight.
A: “The opps talkin bout they finna pull up. I hope they know I aint turnin down nothin.” 

Amp up/Gas up: Similar to ‘go hard’ or ‘turn up’. To put more effort into something.
A: “Yeaite blood! Gas up! Gas up on that nigga blood!” 


Bang Dat: Put that, similar to swearing on something important (i.e On God). Can be used in multiple contexts:
A: “You can bang dat?”
B: “I’m forreal!”

A: “I’m gettin tired of these fake ass niggas tryna claim the hood.” 
B: “Bang dat on God, S’about time we did some about it, cuzz.”

Bleed/Bleeder: To roast somebody. Can be used in multiple contexts:
A: “I know you aint gettin no bitches wit dat hairline cuzz.” 
B: “Damn cuzz! Bleed him!” 

A: *Takes an ugly photograph of someone.*
B: “Oh my God, thass a bleeder!” 

Bunk: Lame or bullshit.
A: “Man them niggas is bunk they dont ever pop out.”

A: “Tha homie on bunk nigga. He trippin trippin.” 

Bust Down: Can mean an expensive watch; that something looks good or it can refer to dancing.
A: “Damn gurr! Yo outfit buss down!”
B: “Bitch! Look at you!” 

A: “I just got my paycheck, I’m finna buy me a buss down.” 
B: “What plays you makin to have money like dat?”

Bussin: Looking good.
A: “Yeaite, that fit bussin.”

(Get on) Bullshit: Get into conflict.
A: “I’m finna get on some bullshit tonight.” 

Blower/burner/foe-feezy/billy/heater/llama: Gun.
A: “Get the blower, cuzz.” 

Bop: In the context of a song it’s a good thing, but in the context of a female its derogatory.
A: “You hear Bino Rideaux new song yet?” 
B: “Yea, it’s a bop!”

A: “Look at what she wearin cuzz!”
B: “Bop!”

Bread/Blue(face)s/Uchies/Cheese/Paper: Money.
A: “Gotta get yo bread up, foo.” 
B: “Dead homies nigga I been workin on some plays.”

Capping/Poppin it: Bullshitting/lying or stretching the truth. Poppin it can also mean being gassed up.
A: “I been gettin hella bitches since I got my new beamer.”
B: “Yeaaite, why you poppin it?”

A: “Cuzz came through poppin it.”
B: “That nigga turnt up.”

Do the Dash/Run track/hit the gas: Common substitutes for running away.
A: “I juss made a snoova nigga do the dash, cuzz!”
B: “Pssht! Those niggas scary.”

Dub: Anything associated with the number two. Two grams of a drug, a $20 bill. Can also be used as an insult.
A: “Can you spot me a dub? I don’t get paid until next week and I gotta buy gas.” 
B: “For sure my nigga.” 

A: “There go that dub ass nigga that was trippin earlier.” 
B: “I’m on that nigga, cuzz. Come on.” 

Dusty: Ugly/worn out.
A: “My shoes dusty, bro. I gotta get me some new ones.” 
B: “For sure. I could use some new shits myself.” 

Earn your stripes/Earn a stripe: To earn your stripes is to prove that you are capable of being from a certain hood. This could mean you chip somebody, mark out an enemy etc. The singular ‘earn a stripe’ means to do something which gains reputation in the streets.
A: “Tired of these lil niggas tryna claim the set n makin us look bad when shit get real.” 
B: “Niggas needa earn they stripes, cuzz. We gotta tighten this shit up.” 

Eyebusters: Most often a whack out, basically a tattoo or symbol which draws attention to you from the enemies. Think of C-Mac’s ‘Hoova Killa’ tattoo.
A: “Cuh ass had the NY whacked out on his face, loc..” 
B: “I peeped, nigga had all the eyebusters..” 

(Non) Factors: To be a factor means you or your gang are important in the streets. Being non-factors means your gang is inactive/not a threat to anyone.
A: “Them niggas is bunc, loc. I aint scared of no nigga from over there.” 
B: “On hood, niggas is non factors.”

(Back) Fade(s): To fight. ‘Taking backfades’ is where if your homie is fighting someone, you have their back by fighting anyone who jumps in/fighting other people who want it.
A: “I’m tryna catch my fade wit that nigga Dee from five deuce.” 
B: “I got backfades then, cuzz. Fuck him and his homies.”

(You) Feel me/Know what I’m sayin: Self explanatory, do you empathise with me.
A: “I’m finna head inside bro I gotta be up early tomorrow for work, you feel me.” 
B: “You good, bro. I’ll hit you up tomorrow.”

Fire/Slaps/Smack(in): Good.
A: “You ever had the Grilled Chicken Club from Chick-Fil-A?” 
B: “Yeah bro. That shit smack.”

Flock: To steal from someone’s home.
A: “I’m tryna flock some shit, blood. A nigga broke.”
B: “On westside, we could get on that.”

Foo/Foolie: Friend/homie.
A: “Wassup foolie?”
B: “Aye cuzz, wassup?”

Forreal: Agreed.
A: “It’s way too hot in here, bro. I’m headin outside.” 
B: “Forreal, lets go.” 

Gang thick: Being deep in numbers.
A: “We gang thick out here, blood.”

Go up/Get off: Like turning up.
A: “We’re finna go up for the homie, blood. Everybody showin out.’ 
B: “On bloods, gone give blood a /good/ send off.” 

A: “Get off cuzz! Uh huh! (Laughs)’ 
B:*Start’s dancing in front of the camera.*

Go (hard): To go crazy at something, and to do it well. Can also just mean that something is good.
A: “What you think of the food?”’ 
B: “Oh yea, that shit go fasho.” 


Good looks/Good lookin (out): Appreciate it/thank you.
A: “I got yo pack in the crib, cuzz. I’ll go grab it for you.” 
B: “Good looks, what’s the ticket on it?” 

Hang your flag up: But your days of gangbanging behind you.
A: “Might as well hang your flag up, blood. You got a whole wife and kids.” 

Heem/H.I.M: The GOAT.
A: “I aint finna lie, I’m heem.” 
B: “Hell naw nigga, you aint heem!” 

Hella/Dumb/Mad: Intensifier, substitute for ‘very’.
A: “That nigga hella corny.” 
B: “On me, he a weirdo.” 

High Tech: Good, of high quality, fly. 
A: *Flexes his new car on Instagram.*
B: *Comments:* ‘This nigga think he high tec.’

Hog: To boss someone around/make them your bitch.
A: “Pass me yo car keys, nigga.” 
B: “Out here hoggin niggas, damn..” 

Hood Hop/Flipping Hoods: To hood hop is to switch hood, most of the time it’s seen to be a bad thing but it depends whether you flip from one side to another. Flipping from one NHC to another NHC gang one time is not really considered bad vs flipping from a NHC gang to Hoover would be heavily frowned upon. Flipping hoods more than once is a no-go.
A: “Peep this cuzz - I swear this nigga was claimin Eight Tray Gangsters last summer...” 
B: “Man that nigga /been/ a hood hopper, cuzz used to claim Nine Deuce Hoover when he was a kid.” 

Issue: Problem. Getting your issue off means to fight to settle a problem.
A: “That nigga been pissin me off, I gotta get my issue off wit bro when I see em.”
B: “It aint no use leavin that shit up inna air, bro. Niggas needa get it in.”

One-Time/Johnnies/The Jakes/The Boys: Police.
A: “I had to run from the jakes!”
B: “You lucky you got away, cuzz.”

Jumpin/Poppin: Lit.
A: “Cuzz, the club was jumpin last night!”
B: “On God. That shit was crazy.”

Keep Dat (Same Energy): Similar to ‘fine, have it your way.’ Multiple contexts:
A: “Those niggas better keep dat same energy when we pull up later.” 
B: “On Crip, those niggas stay poppin it on the net.” 

A: “Nigga, I know for a fact you aint finna pull up.” 
B: “Aite, keep dat.” 

Lackin: Getting caught in a vulnerable situation, i.e without your homies or without your strap.
A: “Those niggas caught me lackin, cuzz.” 
B: “Forreal, we finna get our get back doe. Don’t trip.” 

Laggin/Snoozin: If you’re zoned out or holding someone up.
A: “This nigga laggin.” 
B: “Forreal, we been waitin like ten minutes.” 

Ling Ling Boppin: Popularised by rappers such as Bris, G-Man and Drakeo Tha Ruler, originally a Sacramento term meaning to steal from Chinese people since they don’t use bank accounts (listen to YG - Meet The Flockers for more context). 
A: “I been ling ling boppin, Mei Ling just took me shoppin.” 

Loafin: Not being active, lazing around smoking weed etc.Can be synonymous with ‘lacking’ in certain contexts.
A: “Wassup cuzz, where you comin from?”
B: “Shit I been loafin, thought I’d pop out one time.” 

A: “I caught that snoover nigga loafin, cuzz!”
B: “Tell me you packed that nigga out cuh!”

(Pippi) Longstocking: Again more of a Sac term but has made its way over to LA. It means a gun with an extended magazine.
A: “I'm just tryna rob a ling ling longstocking, ayy
Pull up, pah-pah-pah, you hear that pole poppin, uh.”

Low Cee: Lowkey but Crip. 
A: “That nigga low cee pissing me off.” 
B: “On God, I’m boutta snap.” 

Mark (ass)/Marked Out: Basically like calling someone a bitch, more of a serious insult.
A: “Those niggas from five deuce just caught me lackin, cuzz!” 
B: “Mark ass niggas, cuzz. Get yo car, we in traffic.” 

A: “Fuck that nigga, bro. He marked out.” 

Money plots/plays: Activities which primarily result in making money. 
A: “Man I gotta get my bread up, a nigga tired of takin the bus.” 
B: “I got some money plots I been workin on, foo. Peep this.” 

Mop/Head: The terminology for getting a BJ.
A: “That bitch gave me some good mop doe!” 
B: “Oh yea? I knew she was a lil thooter.” 

Movin Mickey: Means the same as shiesty; shady or suspicious.
A: “Watch that car, blood. Niggas is movin mickey.” 
B: “On bloods, I peeped dat.” 

Nerd/Square/Neutron: Someone who isn’t involved in gangbanging, just a regular citizen.
A: “Snu dat crossin the street?!”
B: “Don’t trip, thass Xavier. He square.” 

Off Top(s): No doubt/First of all.
A: “You tryna head inside the crib? I aint tryna get caught lackin.” 
B: “Off tops, I was finna suggest that myself.” 

Outta pocket: Acting out of order.
A: “My babymomma actin way outta pocket recently.”
B: “You needa get one of the HG’s to pack her out.”

(That’s) Out: Originates from out of fashion, not cool.
A: “You see that shit Tiny Stretch posted on Instagram?”
B: “Yea bro. That shit’s out, I’m finna DP that nigga when I see em.” 

(No) Pass/Passes: Kind of like the hood version of a hall pass i.e you have a pass to enter a certain hood because you know someone from there. To have no passes means no one is allowed to go through your section unscathed regardless of who they know.
A: “Niggas dont play where we from, blood. Walk through the section you might just get yo shit snatched.” 
B: “On bloods, it’s no passes out here nigga.”

Packed/Maxed Out/Blitz/Sleep/Whoop: Beaten up.
A: “Im finna pack that nigga out.” 
B: “You trippin!”

A: “Blitz that nigga cuh!” 
B: “On Crip get him!”

Play Ball: Get active/make moves in the streets.
A: “These snoover niggas aint tryna play ball.” 
B: “On Crip, niggas inactive.” 

Plots: Plans.
A: “What’s plots?” 
B: “Ion know, I was finna head to the store real quick.” 

Politickin: To discuss something gang related.
A: “We still got that other shit we gotta politic about, cuzz. About F-Foe.” 


Put On: To be made a member of the gang, also to show someone something/enlighten them. A 1-Man or 2-Man or 3-Man etc put on determines how many people you had to fight in order to get put on.
A: “Let me put you onto this new YG, bro.”
B: “Say dat, that shit hard?”

Rankin it: Not banging your set/hiding your affiliation.
A: “Why he aint tryna throw his shit up, blood? Stop rankin it, blood.”
B: “I know that nigga from the fonkies, blood.”

Ringing Bells: Making noise around the city - becoming reputable.
A: “Oh shit, wassup foo - yo name been ringin bells around the bity...”
B: “Dead homies, blood. Niggas startin to pay attention.”

Say dat/less: Agreed/Alright.
A: “I’m finna head to the stu, you tryna slide?.”
B: “Say dat, I’ll catch up wit you later I gotta dip.”

Sag/Sagged/Troop/Soldier: Loitered with the intent to get active when it comes to it.
A: “I just sagged the enemies hood, blood, where you at?” 
B: “We on the foe, cuzz.” 

Shiesty: Shady or greedy.
A: “I had my hand on my strap the whole time, blood. Dat shit was lookin shiesty..” 

A: “Man you really not finna let me get a plate? You shiesty for dat, cuzz.” 

Slide: Come through.
A: “Slide over to the crib.”
B: “Say dat, I’m on my way.”

Smoke: Conflict.
A: “Those niggas dont want smoke.” 
B: “On God, niggas know they finna get packed out.”

Squabble/Wobble/Bump: Fight.
A: “Aye if it’s a issue we can bump, cuzz. Ion care.” 
B: “On Bloods squabble up den, foo.”

A: “That nigga got a mean wobble cuzz.” 
B: “Period! Packed his ass out.”

Strip/Jack: To rob somebody.
A: “Aye strip cuh before the jakes show up, nigga!” 
B: *Quickly pats down the enemy gang member.* 

Strike up/Hit up/tag/throw up/block up: Terminology all related to graffiti. Strike ups are the most commonly referred to - a throw up is much quicker/not worried about the visual. Blocking it up refers to writing in block letters.
A: “Aye dat shit hard, block it up too.” 
B: “Dead homies I gotchu holon.” 

Swoop: Pick someone up..
A: “I’ll swoop you in five minutes, be ready.” 
B: “Say less.” 

That’s dead/Kill that: No longer relevant/stop talking about that/put an end to it.
A: “Aye cuzz you remember that female you was fuckin wit from the fifties?”
B: “That’s dead, cuzz. Don’t bring that shit up.”

A: *Starts acting goofy in a serious situation.*
B: “Kill that shit, cuzz.” 

Thot/Thooter: Derogatory to women, obvious.
A: “She’s a real thooter.”

Weird(o)/Holly(weird): A light insult, meaning has softened considerably due to overuse. Holly comes from Hollyweird, originates from the Bay Area but used in LA on occasion particularly by younger people.
A: “That nigga weird.”
B: “I’m sayin.” 

Whack Out: To cross out or put a K behind an enemy hood when striking up.
A: “Aye cuzz, make sure you whack dat foe doe.”
B: “I got it cuzz, holon.


Yackameechie: Means the same as issue. If you got a issue with somebody in LA it means you have beef. Can also mean gun.
A: “I got a lil yackameechie I gotta handle tonight, loc.” 
B: “That nigga from the sissies been talkin shit again? Wassup?.”

Yeaaite: Sarcastic response, whatever.
A: “You a whole ass weirdo, cuzz.” 
B: “Yeaaite.”
